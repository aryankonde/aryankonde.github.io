About

An aspiring data scientist and AI engineer with a strong passion for machine learning, committed to harnessing data-driven insights to tackle complex challenges and foster innovation across diverse industries.

Currently a Technology Analyst at Citi, I build controls automation platforms and integrate intelligent document processing into regulated financial workflows. Previously, at Prescient Technologies, I contributed to an AI Agent Studio for orchestrating multi-agent workflows using LangChain, Agent2Agent, and the Model Context Protocol — and fine-tuned an LLM for Text-to-SQL generation, reaching ~88% token accuracy.

My work bridges agentic AI, machine learning, and full-stack engineering — from voice-driven accounting assistants powered by Whisper.cpp to GenAI-powered graph database migration. I'm always open to collaboration with founders and builders working on impactful AI products.
